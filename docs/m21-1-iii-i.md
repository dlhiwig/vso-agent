# M21-1 Part III, Subpart i — map

Part III is **The Development Process**. Subpart i is **Verification of Service and Claims Development**.

Official TOC: https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000073398/M21-1-Adjudication-Procedures-Manual-Table-of-Contents

M21-1 is VBA procedure. 38 U.S.C. and 38 CFR control if they conflict. KnowVA slugs move. This is not a complete reprint.

## Do not confuse with the pre-2021 book

Before the 2021 reorganization, "Part III, Subpart i" was Veterans Service Center structure, pre-discharge / BDD / IDES, and FDC/DRC. Those articles now live elsewhere (or are marked Historical / Rescinded). Cite the **current** chapter titles below.

## Current structure

### Chapter 1 — Establishing Veteran Status and Verifying Service

| Section | Title | Why a VSO opens it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Establishing Veteran Status | Definition of Veteran / active service / qualifying service |
| B | Service Requirements and Verification of Eligibility | 24-month / full-period minimum under 38 U.S.C. 5303A and 38 CFR 3.12a |
| C | Civilian Groups Approved for Veteran Status | Public Law 95-202 groups |

### Chapter 2 — Claims Development

| Section | Title | Why a VSO opens it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Development Duties and Responsibilities | Who gets the evidence; when duty to assist does **not** attach |
| B | Duty to Notify Under 38 U.S.C. 5103 | Section 5103 notice; often already on the EZ form |
| C | Duty to Assist With Obtaining Records | Federal vs private records; reasonable efforts |
| D | Evidence Requested From the Claimant | 30-day ask / one-year statutory window |
| E | Relevance Determinations | VA only has to seek **relevant** records (Golz) |
| F | Record Maintenance During the Development Process | Internal eFolder / flash work — low value for a post packet |
