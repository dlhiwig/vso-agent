# M21-1 III.i.2.D — Evidence requested from the claimant (excerpt)

Official: https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000033257/M21-1-Part-III-Subpart-i-Chapter-2-Section-D-Evidence-Requested-From-the-Claimant

Pair with 38 CFR 3.109.

When VA writes the claimant for evidence, the letter specifies a 30-day period. The letter also tells the claimant that if evidence is not in within 30 days VA may decide on the record, and that the claimant has one year from the date of VA's request to submit evidence or information to substantiate the claim.

Unless a different period is required by policy or regulation, allow 30 days to respond.

If VA requested private records on the claimant's behalf, do not deny before finishing the private-record steps in III.i.2.C.

If VA already has medical evidence (for example VA treatment records), the rating activity must decide whether that evidence supports an award — development is not an excuse to ignore what is already in the file.

Post packets should mark each item have / requested / missing / va-to-obtain so the accredited officer can see what the 30-day clock will hit.
