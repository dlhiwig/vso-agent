# VFW National Veterans Service — claims assistance

Official: https://www.vfw.org/assistance/va-claims-assistance
Related: https://www.vfw.org/media-and-events/latest-releases/archives/2025/5/we-do-this-daily-and-we-always-do-it-for-free
Publisher: Veterans of Foreign Wars of the United States
Snapshot date: 2026-09-19

VFW National Veterans Service and its network of VA-accredited service officers help veterans and their families file VA disability claims and secure earned benefits at no cost. Accreditation means the representative is trained to the standard set by VA for preparing claims and accessing VA data systems.

VFW accredited service officers assist veterans whether or not the veteran is a VFW member. Assistance covers the process from filing through VA review and any needed follow-up or appeal.

A veteran working with VFW should expect:

- VA Form 21-22 appointing VFW as the organization with power of attorney
- Claim preparation on the proper VA form (commonly 21-526EZ for compensation)
- Organization of evidence, not invented evidence
- Filing and representation by the accredited human officer, not by software

This project is not an official VFW product and does not appoint VFW as POA.
