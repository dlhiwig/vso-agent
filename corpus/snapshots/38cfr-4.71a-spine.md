# 38 CFR 4.71a Schedule of ratings — musculoskeletal system (spine excerpt)

Official: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-4.71a
Reprint: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/38/4.71a
Snapshot date: 2026-09-19
Note: Working excerpt. Confirm current general rating formula on eCFR.

The General Rating Formula for Diseases and Injuries of the Spine evaluates the thoracolumbar and cervical spine primarily on range of motion, muscle spasm / guarding, and unfavorable ankylosis. Intervertebral disc syndrome may alternatively be rated on incapacitating episodes when that formula yields a higher evaluation.

Associated objective neurologic abnormalities (for example radiculopathy) are generally rated separately under the appropriate neurologic diagnostic code when the record supports them.

Painful motion and functional loss under §§ 4.40 and 4.59 still matter on exam. A hypothesized "bad back" in an intake file is not a diagnosis and is not a rating.
