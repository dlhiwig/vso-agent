# VA: How to file a disability claim

Official: https://www.va.gov/disability/how-to-file-claim/
Publisher: U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs
Snapshot date: 2026-09-19

You can file a disability claim online on VA.gov, by mail, in person at a VA regional office, or with the help of an accredited representative such as a Veterans Service Officer.

Starting the disability application declares your intent to file. An accredited representative, like a Veterans Service Officer (VSO), can help you fill out your claim.

Evidence could include:

- VA medical records and hospital records that relate to your claimed condition or that show your rated disability has gotten worse
- Private medical records and hospital reports
- Supporting statements from family, friends, coworkers, clergy, or law enforcement personnel with knowledge about how and when your disability happened or how it got worse

In some cases you may need additional forms, for example if you’re claiming a dependent or applying for aid and attendance.

Use VA Form 21-526EZ, Application for Disability Compensation and Related Compensation Benefits, for compensation claims.

This page does not authorize anyone to file on a veteran’s behalf except an accredited representative or the veteran.
