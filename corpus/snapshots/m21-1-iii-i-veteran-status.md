# M21-1 III.i.1.A — Establishing Veteran Status (excerpt)

Official: https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000181424/M21-1%2C-Part-III%2C-Subpart-i%2C-Chapter-1%2C-Section-A---Establishing-Veteran-Status
Change date noted on KnowVA: September 25, 2024 (page also marked updated May 27, 2025)
Snapshot date: 2026-09-19
Pair with 38 CFR 3.1(d) and 38 U.S.C. 101.

A Veteran is a person who served in the active military, naval, air, or space service, and was discharged or released under conditions other than dishonorable.

Active service includes active duty; any period of active duty for training (ADT) during which the person is disabled or dies from a disease or injury incurred or aggravated in the line of duty; and any period of inactive duty for training (IADT) during which the person is disabled or dies from an injury incurred or aggravated in the line of duty, or from acute myocardial infarction, cardiac arrest, or a cerebrovascular accident that occurred during that training.

Qualifying service is any type of service that qualifies or potentially qualifies a claimant for VA benefits. The primary factor in determining basic eligibility is Veteran status. Live benefits rest on the claimant's Veteran status; survivors benefits rest on the claimant's status as the spouse, parent, or child of a deceased Veteran.

Civilian groups approved for Veteran status are in III.i.1.C (Public Law 95-202 and related).

A post packet that cannot show qualifying service or a discharge other than dishonorable should flag eligibility before drafting a nexus narrative.
