# M21-1 III.i.2.C — Duty to assist with obtaining records (excerpt)

Official: https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000014102/M21-1-Part-III-Subpart-i-Chapter-2-Section-C-Duty-to-Assist-With-Obtaining-Records
KnowVA article ID: 554400000014102
Snapshot date: 2026-09-19
Pair with 38 U.S.C. 5103A and 38 CFR 3.159.

A substantially complete initial or supplemental claim triggers VA's duty to make reasonable efforts to obtain relevant records in Federal custody.

Federal records include, but are not limited to: service treatment records; other service department records (personnel, line-of-duty, inpatient, behavioral health) not already in the STRs; VA medical and other records including Vet Center records and authorized non-VA treatment; SSA records; Public Health Service records; and Department of Labor records.

Reasonable efforts for Federal records means VA continues until the records are obtained, or it is reasonably certain the records do not exist, or further efforts would be futile.

For relevant non-Federal or private records the claimant adequately identifies, VA makes an initial request and at least one follow-up unless the first response says the records do not exist or a follow-up would be futile. The claimant generally must authorize the release (VA Form 21-4142 / 21-4142a).

VA must notify the claimant when it cannot obtain identified private records necessary to the claim (38 U.S.C. 5103A(b); 38 CFR 3.159(e)).

How-to request mechanics live in Part III, Subpart ii — not in this article.
