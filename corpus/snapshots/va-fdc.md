# VA: Fully Developed Claims program

Official: https://www.va.gov/disability/how-to-file-claim/evidence-needed/fully-developed-claims/
Publisher: U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs
Snapshot date: 2026-09-19

If you want to get a faster decision on your disability benefits claim by submitting evidence along with your claim, use the Fully Developed Claims program.

Here’s what you’ll need to do for us to consider your claim fully developed:

- Submit your completed Application for Disability Compensation and Related Compensation Benefits (VA Form 21-526EZ), and
- Submit all the evidence (supporting documents) you have along with your claim, and
- Certify that there’s no more evidence we might need to decide your claim, and
- Go to any VA medical exams that we schedule, if we decide these exams are needed to make a decision on your claim

You’ll need to submit this evidence:

- All private medical records related to the claimed condition
- Any records of medical treatment you’ve received for the claimed condition while serving in the military
- Any military personnel records you have that relate to the claimed condition
- Information about any related health records that you don’t have but that we can request on your behalf from a federal facility

If you think your service records don’t include a description of your disability, you can also submit letters from family members, friends, clergy, law enforcement, or those you served with.

If we determine that we need other non-federal records to make a decision on your claim, we’ll simply remove the claim from the Fully Developed Claims program and process it as a standard claim.

If you turn in more information or evidence after you submit your fully developed claim, we’ll remove your claim from the FDC program and process it as a standard claim.

VA encourages you to work with an accredited VSO representative. These trained professionals can help you file your claim and gather the needed medical records and evidence.
