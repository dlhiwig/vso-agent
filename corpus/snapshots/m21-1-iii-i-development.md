# M21-1 III.i.2.A — Development duties and responsibilities (excerpt)

Pair with 38 CFR 3.159.

Claimants are ultimately responsible for providing VA with evidence to substantiate the claim. VA still must make every effort to request the evidence it needs from records available when the claim is filed, develop additional evidence the claimant or representative identifies, and make reasonable efforts to obtain records held by a Federal custodian and privately held records the claimant identifies with proper authorization.

VA has no duty to assist when there is no reasonable possibility that assistance would substantiate the claim — for example no qualifying service, other legal ineligibility, or a claim that clearly lacks merit.

The accredited VSO still files. This agent does not send development letters.
