# M21-1 III.i.1.B — Service requirements and verification of eligibility (excerpt)

Topics in the live article: minimum active duty service requirements; verification of service and character of discharge; forms of evidence; alternative sources for evidence of service.

## Minimum active duty (frame)

38 CFR 3.12a / 38 U.S.C. 5303A: persons who originally enlisted in a regular component after September 7, 1980, or entered on active duty after October 16, 1981, generally must have completed 24 continuous months of active duty or have been discharged under 10 U.S.C. 1171, unless an exception applies. Delayed-enlistment entry date is the date of entry on active duty, not the oath date.

Someone who fails the minimum-service rule is generally ineligible for VA benefits except service-connected disability or death, Chapter 19 insurance, and certain Chapter 32 refunds.

## What the post should put in the packet

DD-214 or other separation document status (`have` / `requested` / `missing`). Character of discharge if it is not honorable. Do not invent service dates. Character-of-discharge problems go to the accredited VSO / DSO, not the software.
