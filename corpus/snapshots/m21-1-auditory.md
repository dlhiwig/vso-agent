# M21-1 excerpt — conditions of the auditory system (hearing loss and tinnitus)

Official KnowVA: https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-us/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000014464/M21-1-Part-V-Subpart-iii-Chapter-2-Section-B-Conditions-of-the-Auditory-System
Snapshot date: 2026-09-19
Note: M21-1 is adjudication guidance. 38 U.S.C. and 38 CFR control if they conflict. KnowVA pages move; verify the live article.

VA guidance on claims for hearing loss and/or tinnitus looks for sufficient evidence of a current audiological disability or lay evidence of difficulty hearing, plus evidence of hearing loss or tinnitus in service or an in-service event, injury, disease, or symptoms potentially related to an audiological disability.

A claim for hearing loss is treated as lay evidence that the claimant experiences difficulty hearing. A claim for tinnitus is treated as lay evidence of ringing or similar symptoms.

Sensorineural hearing loss and tinnitus are treated in this article as organic diseases of the nervous system and as subject to presumptive service connection under 38 CFR 3.309(a) when the other presumptive rules are met.

Where service connection is at issue, an audiometric examination and/or medical opinion is requested when necessary under 38 CFR 3.159(c)(4). The Duty MOS Noise Exposure Listing is an internal rating aid; combat and MOS noise are weighed with the rest of the record.

This agent does not assign a rating under DC 6100 or DC 6260.
