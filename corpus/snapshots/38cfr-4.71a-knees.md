# 38 CFR 4.71a Schedule of ratings — musculoskeletal system (knees excerpt)

Official: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-38/section-4.71a
Reprint: https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/38/4.71a
Snapshot date: 2026-09-19
Note: Diagnostic codes and percentage criteria are amended from time to time. This is a working excerpt for packet citations, not a rating calculator.

Common knee diagnostic codes on the musculoskeletal schedule:

- 5256 Knee, ankylosis of (ratings rise with more unfavorable angle)
- 5257 Knee, other impairment of: recurrent subluxation or lateral instability (slight / moderate / severe)
- 5258 Cartilage, semilunar, dislocated, with frequent episodes of "locking," pain, and effusion into the joint
- 5259 Cartilage, semilunar, removal of, symptomatic
- 5260 Leg, limitation of flexion of the knee
- 5261 Leg, limitation of extension of the knee
- 5262 Tibia and fibula, impairment of
- 5263 Genu recurvatum (acquired, traumatic, with weakness and insecurity in weight-bearing objectively demonstrated)

Also read with § 4.40 (functional loss) and § 4.59 (painful motion / minimum compensable rating for a painful joint). Separate evaluations for instability and limitation of motion may be available when the record supports distinct manifestations. The accredited rater — not this agent — assigns the code and percentage.
