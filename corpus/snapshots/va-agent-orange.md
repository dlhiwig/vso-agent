# Agent Orange — PACT Act additions and existing presumption frame

Official: https://www.va.gov/resources/the-pact-act-and-your-va-benefits/
Related: https://www.va.gov/disability/eligibility/hazardous-materials-exposure/
Snapshot date: 2026-09-19
Note: This is not the full Agent Orange condition list. Open the official Agent Orange eligibility page before citing a condition as presumptive.

## Conditions VA publicly added under PACT Act

- High blood pressure (hypertension)
- Monoclonal gammopathy of undetermined significance (MGUS)

VA states other longstanding Agent Orange presumptive conditions include certain cancers, type 2 diabetes, and other illnesses. Confirm each condition on the live VA list.

## Locations VA publicly added under PACT Act

- Any U.S. or Royal Thai military base in Thailand from January 9, 1962, through June 30, 1976
- Laos from December 1, 1965, through September 30, 1969
- Cambodia at Mimot or Krek, Kampong Cham Province from April 16, 1969, through April 30, 1969
- Guam or American Samoa or territorial waters from January 9, 1962, through July 31, 1980
- Johnston Atoll or a ship that called there from January 1, 1972, through September 30, 1977

VA notes this is not the full list of Agent Orange presumptive locations (Republic of Vietnam and Korean DMZ pathways remain).

## Radiation response efforts added (related PACT expansion)

Cleanup of Enewetak Atoll (Jan 1, 1977–Dec 31, 1980); Palomares, Spain B-52 response (Jan 17, 1966–Mar 31, 1967); Thule Air Force Base, Greenland B-52 fire response (Jan 21, 1968–Sep 25, 1968).
