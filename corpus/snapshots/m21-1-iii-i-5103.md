VA shall provide the claimant, by the most effective means available, notice of any information and medical or lay evidence not previously provided that is necessary to substantiate the claim (Section 5103 notice).

VA now often delivers that notice at the time of claim submission, including by incorporating it into the standard EZ application forms, rather than only as a later paper letter. Public Law 112-154 gave VA more flexibility on how and when the notice is delivered.

VA is not required to provide a new Section 5103 notice for claims received within one year of a prior Section 5103 notice that already contained sufficient notice, per 38 U.S.C. 5103(b)(4).

A VSO assembling a packet should still list missing evidence even when the 5103 box was already checked on the EZ form.
