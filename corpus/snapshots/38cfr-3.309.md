# 38 CFR 3.309 Disease subject to presumptive service connection (excerpt)

## (a) Chronic diseases (selected frame)

If a listed chronic disease becomes manifest to a degree of 10 percent or more within the presumptive period in § 3.307, service connection may be granted even without evidence of the disease in service, unless the presumption is rebutted. The list includes, among others, arthritis, diabetes mellitus, organic diseases of the nervous system, and other enumerated chronic diseases. Sensorineural hearing loss and tinnitus are treated in VA adjudication guidance as organic diseases of the nervous system for this purpose — confirm against current M21-1 and the regulation text before relying on that classification.
