# PACT Act / burn pit and toxic exposure presumptive conditions (VA public list)

Official overview: https://www.va.gov/resources/the-pact-act-and-your-va-benefits/
Related: https://www.va.gov/disability/eligibility/hazardous-materials-exposure/
Public Health PACT page: https://www.publichealth.va.gov/exposures/benefits/PACT_Act.asp
Snapshot date: 2026-09-19
Note: Lists change. Verify the live VA page. Not a rating table.

## Presumptive cancers commonly listed for burn pit / toxic exposure (Gulf War and post-9/11 pathways)

Brain cancer; gastrointestinal cancer of any type; glioblastoma; head cancer of any type; kidney cancer; lymphoma of any type; melanoma; neck cancer of any type; pancreatic cancer; reproductive cancer of any type. Public Health also lists acute and chronic leukemias; lymphatic cancer of any type; multiple myelomas; myelodysplastic syndromes; myelofibrosis; urinary bladder, ureter, and related genitourinary cancers.

## Presumptive illnesses commonly listed

Asthma diagnosed after service; chronic bronchitis; chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD); chronic rhinitis; chronic sinusitis; constrictive bronchiolitis or obliterative bronchiolitis; emphysema; granulomatous disease; interstitial lung disease (ILD); pleuritis; pulmonary fibrosis; sarcoidosis.

## Location / era examples VA publishes for these presumptions

On or after September 11, 2001: Afghanistan, Djibouti, Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon, Syria, Uzbekistan, Yemen, and the airspace above those locations.

On or after August 2, 1990: Bahrain, Iraq, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Somalia, the United Arab Emirates, and the airspace above those locations.

Operations often listed: Enduring Freedom, Freedom's Sentinel, Iraqi Freedom, New Dawn, Inherent Resolve, Resolute Support Mission.

Meeting a location list is not the same as a grant. Diagnosis plus qualifying service still have to be in the record. The VSO checks both.
