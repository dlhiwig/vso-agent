# M21-1 III.i.1.B — Service requirements and verification (excerpt)

Official: https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000181425/M21-1-Part-III-Subpart-i-Chapter-1-Section-B-Service-Requirements-and-Verification-of-Eligibility
Change date noted on KnowVA for the minimum-service topic: August 2, 2022 (page also marked updated Dec 28, 2025)
Snapshot date: 2026-09-19
Pair with 38 U.S.C. 5303A and 38 CFR 3.12a.

The minimum active duty service requirement of 38 CFR 3.12a(a)(1) must be met, under 38 U.S.C. 5303A, by persons who originally enlisted in a regular component after September 7, 1980, or entered on active duty after October 16, 1981, and have not previously completed a continuous period of active duty of at least 24 months, and have not been discharged or released under 10 U.S.C. 1171.

A Veteran may meet the minimum period without completing 24 months. 38 CFR 3.12a(a)(1) defines the minimum period as 24 months of continuous active duty, **or the full period for which the person was called or ordered to active duty**.

For delayed enlistment, the entry date is the date of entry on active duty, not an earlier swearing-in date.

A person who does not meet the minimum is generally ineligible for benefits under title 38 except benefits for or in connection with a service-connected disability or death, certain insurance under chapter 19, and certain chapter 32 contribution refunds.

Exceptions exist (hardship, disability, etc.). Read 38 CFR 3.12a and the live III.i.1.B article before telling a veteran they have no claim.
