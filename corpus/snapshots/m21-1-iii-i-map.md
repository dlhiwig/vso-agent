# M21-1 Part III, Subpart i — Verification of Service and Claims Development

Official TOC: https://www.knowva.ebenefits.va.gov/system/templates/selfservice/va_ssnew/help/customer/locale/en-US/portal/554400000001018/content/554400000073398/M21-1-Adjudication-Procedures-Manual-Table-of-Contents
Snapshot date: 2026-09-19
Note: M21-1 is adjudication guidance. 38 U.S.C. and 38 CFR control. KnowVA slugs move.

Part III is "The Development Process." Subpart i is the front door: prove the person is a Veteran, then develop the claim.

## Chapter 1 — Establishing Veteran status and verifying service

- Section A — Establishing Veteran status
- Section B — Service requirements and verification of eligibility (minimum active duty under 38 U.S.C. 5303A / 38 CFR 3.12a; character of discharge; forms of evidence)
- Section C — Civilian groups approved for Veteran status

## Chapter 2 — Claims development

- Section A — Development duties and responsibilities (who obtains evidence; when VA has no duty to assist)
- Section B — Duty to notify under 38 U.S.C. 5103 (Section 5103 notice)
- Section C — Duty to assist with obtaining records (Federal vs private)
- Section D — Evidence requested from the claimant
- Section E — Relevance determinations (Golz: only relevant records)
- Section F — Record maintenance during development (eFolder / VBMS internals — low value for a post packet)

Subpart ii (next door) is how VA actually requests Federal, SSA, and private records. Do not confuse III.i with III.ii.
